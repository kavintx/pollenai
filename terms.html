3. Tasks and uploads

Each task in the app says what to capture, how much each approved upload pays, and how many uploads one person can send. Everything you upload must be:

  • captured by you, in real life, with the Pollen app;
  • not generated or materially altered with AI, not copied, downloaded or photographed off a screen;
  • not something you've uploaded to Pollen before;
  • something you have the right to license — no one else's copyrighted work, and nothing illegal;
  • free of other people's personal information (documents, screens, addresses, license plates).

Faces and voices. If an upload shows a recognisable face or contains a recognisable voice (yours or anyone else's), you must say so when you upload it, turn on that option in your profile, and have the permission of every recognisable person in it. Never include anyone under 18.

4. Review

We review every upload before paying for it. Review is partly automatic: we check for duplicates, basic quality (focus, lighting, silence, length) and, using an AI model, whether it matches the task. Clear passes are approved automatically, clear failures are declined with a reason, and anything uncertain is checked by a person. A declined upload isn't paid or licensed, is deleted, and you can try again with a new one. Our decision on whether an upload meets a task's requirements is final, but if you think we got it wrong, email us.

6. Pay
