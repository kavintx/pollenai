The 48 hours

The clock starts.
It doesn't stop.

One room, one deadline, no off-switch. Here's how the weekend runs, from opening ceremony to Sunday Finals.

  1. 01Day 1 · Morning

    Doors open

    Check in, form teams and settle in for the opening ceremony keynotes and challenge track pitches.

  2. 02Day 1 · Build

    Ship the first version

    Partner teams are on the floor with their APIs, SDKs and challenge tracks. Get something working before midnight.

  3. 03Overnight · 24/7

    The venue stays open

    18+ builders keep going all night. Under-18 builders follow supervised youth rest and chaperone rules.

  4. 04Day 2 · Lock-in

    Submit and rehearse

    Submissions close. Final mentor passes, demo prep and judging checks before the room fills.

  5. 05Day 2 · Sunday

    Finals on stage

    The $25,000 Grand Prize and four $10,000 challenge tracks are judged live in front of the room.

Exact times and the final San Francisco venue are published here once confirmed.

All ages

Everyone builds. Everyone goes home safe.

High school, university and independent builders share the same floor and the same prize pool. The overnight rules are strict on purpose.

18+

  • Standard adult check-in

Supervised, not sidelined.

  • Signed parental consent waiver required
  • Distinct wristband worn at all times
  • Supervised youth rest zones
  • Chaperone rules apply throughout
