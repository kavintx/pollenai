What gets approved.

Buyers pay for real moments. Follow the shot list and these rules, and your work gets through review.

We pay for

  • Photos, video and sound you captured yourself
  • Shots that match the shot list
  • In focus and well lit
  • Real places and real noise

We don't pay for

  • AI-generated or AI-edited content
  • Screenshots, reposts or other people's work
  • Filler like blank walls, ceilings or floors
  • Blurry, dark or near-duplicate shots

Your files. Your call.

Uploading isn't selling. Nothing is licensed until you say so, file by file.

  • Delete what hasn't sold.

Questions.

Something else? contact@usepollen.ai

Who buys the data?

AI companies and research labs that need real-world photo, video and audio to train and test their models.

How much can I make?

Tasks pay $1.75 to $60 each when approved, depending on what's asked. Rates are estimates until launch and aren't guaranteed.

How does the resale royalty work?

Each time a dataset with your file resells, you get 10% of what you were originally paid for that task.

How do I get paid?

Approved pay goes to your Pollen balance. Once you have $25, cash out to your bank through Stripe.

Will buyers see who I am?

No. Datasets never include your name, phone number or date of birth. You show up only as a random ID, and we strip location data from photos.

Who can join?

Anyone 18 or older. One person, one account.

When does it launch?

There's no date yet. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when it opens.
